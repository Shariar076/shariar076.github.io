Attribution graphs read by a circuit oracle reach 50% on the misreported-tool-calls task, basically chance, while a single backward pass reaches 85%. But when the attribution target is redesigned with learned probe directions, the oracle climbs to 80%. The lesson is about question formulation, not tool capability.
